Questions people actually ask.

Can a lottery calculator predict winning numbers?

No. Nothing can. Lottery draws are independent, so past results do not change future odds. A real calculator shows your probability and expected value — not predictions.

What are the odds of winning Powerball?

The jackpot odds are 1 in 292,201,338 (5 of 69 white balls plus the Powerball from 1–26). The odds of winning any prize are about 1 in 24.87.

What are the odds of winning Mega Millions?

The jackpot odds are 1 in 290,472,336 (5 of 70 white balls plus the Mega Ball from 1–24).

Is buying a lottery ticket worth it?

For almost every draw, the expected value of a ticket is negative — you are buying entertainment, not an investment. Only an unusually large jackpot can briefly push EV toward break-even.

How is expected value (EV) calculated?

EV sums the probability of each prize tier times its payout, then subtracts the ticket price. For the jackpot we also model the chance that other winners split it with you.
